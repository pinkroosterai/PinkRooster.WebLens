Description

The Trailhead 38 is built for three- to five-day trips where every ounce matters but you still want the organization of a full-size pack. A single aluminum stay and a contoured foam back panel keep the load close to your spine, even when the pack is loaded near its 40-pound rated capacity.

Two zippered hip belt pockets keep snacks and a phone within reach, while a top lid pocket separates small essentials from the main compartment. Side compression straps double as attachment points for trekking poles or a foam pad, and a dedicated hydration sleeve fits reservoirs up to three liters.

The 38-liter main body is made from a 210-denier recycled ripstop shell with a durable water-repellent finish, backed by a lifetime warranty against manufacturing defects. Available in three colorways: Slate Grey, Moss Green, and Rust.
